## Engagement Strategy Reflection

### Highest Priority Stakeholder

The RCHN Chief Medical Officer requires the most deliberate engagement because this stakeholder has both high interest and high influence. Major clinical workflow changes can affect patient care and clinic operations, and the CMO may need to review or approve important decisions. If the CMO's concerns or information needs are overlooked, the project could face delayed approvals, workflow changes, or resistance to implementation. NovaTech should communicate regularly with the CMO and involve this stakeholder when major clinical decisions are being considered.

### Risk of Omission

One stakeholder group I considered but did not make a major focus of the register is patients. Patients are affected by the accuracy and reliability of the intake and scheduling system, but they are not currently responsible for project decisions or implementation activities. I would add patients as a more active stakeholder group if NovaTech begins conducting patient research, usability testing, or if the project scope expands to include patient-facing features such as an online scheduling portal. Their feedback would then become more important for understanding the patient experience.
