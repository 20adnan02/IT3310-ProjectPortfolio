# Week 3: User Persona and Product Vision

## User Persona

**Persona Name:** Maria

**Staff Role:** Intake Coordinator

**Years in Role:** 3–7 years

**Tech Comfort Level:** Medium, comfortable with common tools

### Primary Work Goal

Register patients and schedule appointments quickly and accurately without having to repeat the same work.

### Main Frustration with the Current System

The current system requires too many screens to complete patient registration, which slows down the process and makes daily work more difficult.

### What Would Make Their Work Better

A simpler system that keeps patient information together, reduces repeated data entry, and clearly confirms when an appointment has been saved.

## Customer Needs

### Need 1 – Efficiency

**User Type:** Intake Coordinator

Intake coordinators need a faster and easier way to complete patient registration because the current system requires too many screens. Addressing this matters because it would save time, reduce unnecessary steps, and make the intake process smoother for both staff and patients.

### Need 2 – Reliability

**User Type:** Clinic Manager

Clinic managers need a more reliable and shared scheduling system across all RCHN locations. Right now, each clinic works separately, and staff sometimes cannot confirm whether appointments were saved correctly. Addressing this would help reduce double bookings and make scheduling more consistent across the clinics.

## Product Vision Statement

For RCHN intake coordinators who need a faster and more reliable way to manage patient intake and scheduling, a unified patient intake and scheduling system will make daily workflows easier, reduce scheduling errors, and improve consistency across RCHN clinics.

## Product Vision Reflection

I chose intake coordinators because the research shows that they have to go through too many screens to complete patient registration. The research also shows problems with appointments not saving correctly and staff having to repeat work. I chose a unified intake and scheduling system because it can make the process easier and more reliable. The outcome provides value to RCHN by saving staff time, reducing scheduling errors, and creating a more consistent workflow across the clinics.
